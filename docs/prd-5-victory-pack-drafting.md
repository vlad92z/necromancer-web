# Victory Card Rewards PRD

## Rules

- Defeating an enemy presents three distinct random cards from that enemy's drop pool.
- Click a card to select it; click the selected card again to deselect it.
- Selection is staged only. `Return to Map` adds the selected card to the base deck; returning with no selection skips the reward.
- The active encounter clears only after returning to the map.

## Goblin Drop Pool

- Throw Rock — Life, common, 1 mana, deal 3 damage.
- Hide — Life, common, 0 mana, consume a rune to gain 3 armor.
- Scorch — Fire, common, 3 mana, at end of your turn consume a Life rune to deal 5 damage.
- Lifeline — Life, uncommon, 5 mana, increase maximum health by 5.

## UI

- Show three full card views, matching the hand tooltip card language.
- Selected card uses the yellow selected-card highlight.
- Explain that deselection and skipping are possible.

## Removed

- Six type packs, hidden pack contents, rarity rolling, uncommon guarantees, draft counters, and immediate deck mutation.
