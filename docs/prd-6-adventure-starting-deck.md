# Adventure Starting Deck

## Cards

- 2 Frost Shield, common Frost, 2 mana: Shield 3.
- 2 Firebolt, common Fire, 1 mana: deal 2 damage.
- 2 Lightning Bolt, common Lightning, 2 mana: deal 7 damage when consumed, destroyed, or transformed.
- 2 Barricade, common Life: Shield 5.
- 2 Void Tendrils, common Void, 3 mana: consume adjacent runes, deal 2 damage for each rune consumed.
- 1 Tornado, common Wind, 5 mana: deal 10 damage.
- 1 Headwind, uncommon Wind, 4 mana: Consume a random Wind Rune to reduce incoming damage by 5.

The player starts each turn with 7 mana and draws 5 cards.

Headwind is the deck's only uncommon card. All other starting cards are common.

## Goblin Encounter

- The Goblin starts at 20 health.
- Its 5x5 board is neutral and accepts any rune type.
- Each turn it places three Throw Rock cards and one Hide card.
- Throw Rock is common Life, uses the Life token, costs 1 mana, and deals 3 damage. Hide uses its dedicated art, costs 0 mana, consumes a rune, and gives its own token Shield 2.
